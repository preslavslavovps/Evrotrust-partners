Evrotrust Services

Qualified Trust Service Provider (QTSP) — fully eIDAS compliant across the EU

eID — Electronic Identification
Remote identity verification via mobile app. Basic ID, Enhanced ID (Web SDK), and Full eID with KYC. No physical presence needed. Active users from 62 nationalities.
eSign — Electronic Signature
SES, AES and QES — legally equivalent to handwritten signature across all EU. Signing documents remotely via mobile with same legal effect as paper.
eDelivery — Registered E-mail
Securely send documents with full legal proof of sending, delivery and opening. Qualified timestamp receipts — court evidentiary value.
eTimestamp — Qualified Timestamp
Proves a document existed at a specific point in time. Tamper-proof, legally binding under eIDAS. 0.02 TU per timestamp.
eSeal — Electronic Seal
Qualified electronic seals for organizations — ensures authenticity of documents issued by legal entities. eIDAS Art. 35 compliant.
White-label SDK
Embed Evrotrust services in your platform. Mobile SDK (iOS, Android, React) and Web SDK. Plug-and-play, fully customizable UX.
Mobile SDK
Native iOS, Android and React SDK. Most secure — private key stored in device HSM, never leaves device. Full KYC video flow, biometric authentication.
Trust Units (TU)
Prepaid packages valid 12 months. 1 TU = QES doc · 0.7 TU = QES hash · 3 TU = eID · 1 TU = eDelivery · 0.02 TU = timestamp. Certificates free.
AML / KYC Compliance
Full eIDAS, GDPR and AML-compliant remote identification. KYC onboarding for banks, insurance, telecom and financial services.

Evrotrust is a European Qualified Trust Service Provider (QTSP) officially listed in the EU Trusted List. As a leading provider of digital identity and electronic signature solutions, we enable businesses across Europe to conduct secure, legally compliant digital transactions without the need for physical presence. Our offering delivers fully remote, secure, and eIDAS compliant services designed specifically for regulated industries including banking, insurance, telecommunications, healthcare and other.
