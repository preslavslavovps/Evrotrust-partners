How to use this page

Search & Filter
Type in the search box to find partners by name, region or category. Use the category pills to filter by type.
Open partner details
Click any card to see full use case, integrated services, business model and related partners. On desktop — hover 2 seconds to auto-open.
❤ Save favorites
Click the heart icon on any card to save it. View all saved partners under the "❤ Saved" tab — great for preparing before a meeting.
⇄ Compare partners
Click the arrows icon on 2 cards to compare them side by side — category, use case, integrations and more.
🗺 Map view
Switch to map view with the map icon to see partners by country. Click a dot to see who is there.
📄 Fact sheets & Legal
Scroll down for downloadable fact sheets for each Evrotrust service, and country-by-country legal signing requirements across Europe.
🎨 Themes
Use the color dots in the top navigation to switch between Ocean Blue, Forest, Sunset and Midnight themes.
