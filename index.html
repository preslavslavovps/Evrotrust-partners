What is Evrotrust?

Evrotrust is a European digital trust provider operating at the intersection of law and technology, enabling legally recognized digital identity and trust relationships in a fully remote environment.

It represents a trust infrastructure, not just a platform — combining regulatory compliance, cryptography, and secure identity management into a single ecosystem.

⚖️
Legal-tech infrastructure that transforms identity into a secure, digital, and legally binding asset
🔗
Trust without physical interaction — enabling binding agreements fully remotely
🌍
Pan-EU recognition — legally valid across all EU member states

Legal Architecture
01
Identity Assurance
Legal validation of a person's identity remotely — equivalent to physical identification under regulated procedures
02
Trust Services
Creation of legally recognized digital actions — binding agreements without physical presence
03
Evidence & Auditability
Every action is traceable, timestamped, and legally verifiable in court
Technological Foundation
PKI Infrastructure
Each user has a private key (user-controlled) and public key (for verification). Enables authentication, data integrity, and non-repudiation.
Cryptographic Security
Asymmetric encryption, digital certificates, secure key storage (QSCD). Data cannot be altered, identity cannot be forged, transactions are tamper-proof.
Remote Verification
Biometric verification (face matching, liveness detection), document authenticity checks, AI-assisted validation. No physical presence required.
Key Principles
Decentralized trust logic · Zero physical dependency · Interoperability across systems, countries & regulations · Security by design
